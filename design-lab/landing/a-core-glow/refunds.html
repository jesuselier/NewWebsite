Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026 Draft · review before launch

How to cancel, what happens to your access, and when we give money back.

The short version

  • Cancel anytime in Whop. You won't be charged again.
  • You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • We don't give partial refunds for a month or year that has already started, except in the cases below.
  • Charged twice or by mistake? Tell us within 14 days and we will refund it. [CONFIRM: 14 days]
  • Yearly plan: ask within 7 days of buying it and get a full refund. [CONFIRM: 7 days, and whether to add a usage limit]
  • Founding members keep $49 a month as long as they stay subscribed. If you cancel and rejoin after prices go up, you pay the price at that time.
  • We always give any refund the law requires.

01How to cancel

Log in to your Whop account, open your Swarm membership and cancel it there. You can do this anytime, on any plan.

To avoid the next charge, cancel before your renewal date. If you have trouble cancelling, email us and we will do it for you.

Leaving the Discord server or deleting your Discord account does not cancel your plan. Only cancelling in Whop, or asking us to, stops the payments.

02What happens after you cancel

  • Your plan won't renew, and you won't be charged again.
  • You keep full access until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • When that period ends, your member access in Discord ends too.
  • You can rejoin anytime at the price at that time.

03Founding members

The first 50 founding members pay $49 a month. As long as you don't cancel, you keep $49 a month, even after prices go up.

If you cancel and rejoin after prices have gone up, you pay the price at that time, not $49.

04Monthly plans

This covers Founding Monthly ($49) and Standard Monthly ($99).

We don't give refunds or partial refunds for a month that has already started. If Swarm isn't right for you, cancel and you won't be charged for the next month.

05Yearly plan

The Yearly plan costs $990 a year.

  • You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase. [CONFIRM: 7-day window, and whether to add a usage limit]
  • After that, we don't give prorated refunds for the rest of the year. If you cancel, you keep access until the year ends, and the plan won't renew.

06Duplicate or mistaken charges

If you were charged twice, or charged by mistake (for example after you cancelled), email us within 14 days of the charge. We will check it and refund the charge. [CONFIRM: 14 days]

07If we end your membership

If we remove you for breaking our Terms of Service, your plan stops renewing and we don't refund the rest of that billing period, unless the law requires it.

If we close Swarm entirely, we will give members notice and refund any prepaid time that is left. [CONFIRM: refund on closure]

08Refunds the law requires

If the law where you live gives you a right to a refund, we will always honor it. Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under consumer protection law.

09How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, through Whop. Depending on your bank, a refund can take several business days to show up. Bank transfers (ACH) can take longer than cards.

If we refund the period you are in now, for example a Yearly refund in the first 7 days, your access ends when the refund is made. Refunding a duplicate charge doesn't change your access.

10Whop's terms

Checkout runs through Whop, and Whop's own buyer terms also apply to your purchase.

If something looks wrong with a charge, please email us before you open a dispute with your bank. We can usually fix it faster.

Ask for a refund

Email us from the address you used at checkout. Tell us which charge it is and why, and include your Whop receipt if you have it.

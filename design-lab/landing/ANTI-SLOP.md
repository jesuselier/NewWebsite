# Anti-slop checklist for the Swarm launch page

Any item below fails a page, whatever else it does well. Each one can be checked on a screenshot or in the page source. Jesus asked for the centered, reference-like flow of joinasymmetric.xyz, so a centered layout is expected here and is not a failure on its own.

## Brand kit
- Gold anywhere other than the mark's core cell, one primary button per view, and one highlighted number per view.
- A second lit cell, a rebuilt or redrawn Hive Core, a recolored or stretched logo SVG, or the word SWARM typed instead of using the SVG.
- Fonts other than Unbounded 600 (headlines), JetBrains Mono 500 (labels, numbers), Hanken Grotesk (body, UI). Paragraphs set in Unbounded.
- Drop shadows, glows on text or buttons, glassmorphism, or gradients other than the core cell's glow.
- Decorative patterns other than the honeycomb field. Square grids, dot grids, noise blobs, beams.
- Grey `#55555B` used for text.
- Border radius that ignores the kit (28 panels, 18 buttons and inputs, 10 chips), or `rounded-lg` everywhere.

## Content and honesty
- Any number not in the brief (only $49, $99, $990, 50 seats, 2 months free, 2021, 2023, 4:05, three cycles, and video timestamps are allowed) without a dated source line.
- Testimonials, member counts, seats-left counts, wins, returns, P&L, "value" dollar figures, or prices of assets.
- Mocks without `ILLUSTRATION · NOT LIVE DATA`, or mocks showing tickers, prices, percentages, BUY or SELL.
- Copy that is not grounded in the intro video, the pricing, or the brief. Wording lifted from joinasymmetric.xyz.
- Em dashes or en dashes anywhere in visible text.
- Headlines that label ("Our Features", "Pricing") instead of saying something.

## Layout and craft
- Three or four equal cards with an icon on top as the "features" section.
- Emoji or icon-pack icons as content. Coin logos floating in space.
- Neon particle-network "blockchain" backgrounds, purple or blue gradients.
- The first gold CTA not visible in the first screen at 1440x900 or 390x844.
- Horizontal scrolling on a phone, clipped text, overlapping elements, unreadable contrast.
- Text drawn into a canvas or WebGL scene.
- Motion that never settles, has no reduced-motion still, keeps running offscreen, or blocks reading the hero.
- A page that looks finished only after scrolling: the first screen must read as complete at rest.

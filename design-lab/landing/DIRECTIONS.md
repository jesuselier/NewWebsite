Written October 16, 2026, after Jesus asked for the page to look and flow like joinasymmetric.xyz. ## Direction A: Core Glow (closest to the reference)

**Idea:** the Asymmetric hero energy, rebuilt as the Hive Core. Light comes from one gold cell above the headline instead of beams.

- **Hero signature (2D Canvas, the strongest "agents at work" read):** full-bleed canvas behind the hero, honeycomb field fading out from a core placed top-center (about 0.5W, 0.24H desktop; 0.5W, 0.2H phone). 260 agents desktop / 90 phone scan independently, ping cells, then reach consensus and stream into the core (timeline in `RESEARCH-NOTES.md`, all settled by 4.6s). As they arrive, a soft warm falloff opens around the core (the "light from above"), and the official `swarm-mark.svg` fades in exactly on the lit cell. Afterwards a few scouts keep patrolling at low opacity with a pause control. Start from `proto/converge-2d.html` but make it bolder: brighter trails (`#EEEBE4` committed at 0.6 alpha), larger heads (2.5px), denser near the core, and a clear arrival moment.
- **Curve piece (2D):** "The crowd": a 1px bell curve that draws once, with agents packed under it as a beeswarm behaving per zone: LEFT agents herd in one drifting heading, MID agents twitch and keep stepping out to the baseline, RIGHT agents hold still and oriented, then step out together. A "YOU" tick rides left to right with scroll.
- **3D piece:** inside the Custom AI tools card, a small Three.js scene: an instanced hex patch seen at an angle where scanning cells lift and settle as agents pass, ending flat with one lit cell. Lazy-loaded when the card is visible.
- **Mocks:** agent console (rows with mono ids SCOUT 01 to 04, verb plus object, six-dot hex spinner, states from the fixed vocabulary), watch list with redaction bars, live call card with an animated audio bar strip and `NEXT CALL [DATA NEEDED]`, trader room with redacted messages and cycle tags.
- **Treatment:** the most Asymmetric-like. Floating quote cards slightly tilted (about 2 degrees), pill eyebrows everywhere, generous glow only at the core.

## Direction B: Landing (cinematic 3D)

**Idea:** a 4.6 second drone shot. The camera skims low over a dark plain of hex plates, cranes up and lands top-down on the one lit cell as the Hive Core appears, then the centered hero copy sits over a calm field.

- **Hero signature (Three.js r160):** world units equal CSS px at the final camera. An InstancedMesh of extruded hex plates (radius 0.97R, R 34 desktop, 15 rings, about 721 cells; R 22 phone, 10 rings) with baked top and side tones on `#111114`, an InstancedMesh of rims (`#1C1C21`, sensed cells `#55555B`), and agent ticks (360 desktop / 120 phone) as instanced short strokes oriented by velocity. Camera path: low glancing angle, crane and tilt from 52 to 0 degrees, finishing before 3.9s, never moving once the SVG shows. Plates lift slightly where agents sense, then settle. Core cell turns gold at 3.9s; the official mark fades in on it. Hero text is legible over the field from the first frame (it sits where the field is darkest). Fallback to the 2D version if WebGL fails, and a still under reduced motion. Start from `proto/converge-3d.html`.
- **Curve piece (2D):** the same crowd-under-the-curve idea, staged as three lanes (left, mid, right) with their behaviors, drawn on one canvas.
- **Second 3D moment:** in "Why now", a band where a current of agents drifts from a `2020 · GAMES` basin to a `SINCE 2023 · AI` basin and lands on the core (labels are DOM).
- **Mocks:** decision trace (SVG: six SCOUT branches converge on one node, five prune to `#232328`, the survivor lands as a members-only watch list update made of redaction bars), watch list, live call card, trader room. Cards have a slight 3D tilt toward the pointer on desktop (max 4 degrees, off on touch and reduced motion).
- **Treatment:** more cinematic and spacious; fewer floating cards (one quote card), bigger hero.

## Direction C: Three Cycles (founder-led 3D)

**Idea:** his story is the motion. Three honeycomb planes hang in depth, labelled 2021, 2023 and NOW; agents fall through them, and a dolly zoom collapses the parallax until all three lock into one field and the Hive Core lands. Three cycles become one view, which is the promise: "I want to get you there in one."

- **Hero signature (Three.js r160):** three pointy-top honeycomb planes merged into one LineSegments draw (NOW at z 0, 2023 at z 0.18H, 2021 at z 0.36H; 9 rings desktop, 7 phone), each plane's lattice pre-scaled so all three register exactly from the final eye. Agents (300 desktop / 110 phone) as instanced strokes falling along their velocity. The FOV animates (dolly zoom) so the planes slide into registration by 3.9s; the core charges; the mark fades in. DOM cycle labels ride their planes and fade as they merge. Fallback and reduced-motion still as in B. Use `proto/converge-sdf.html` or `converge-3d.html` as a starting point for the field and timing.
- **Curve piece (2D):** "The crowd" beeswarm, as in A, with the zones labelled `LEFT CURVE · 1ST CYCLE`, `MID CURVE · 2ND CYCLE`, `RIGHT CURVE · 3RD CYCLE`, tying it to the hero's three planes.
- **Founder section is the centerpiece:** a large portrait, the transcript card, and a horizontal three-node timeline (2021, 2023, NOW) whose line draws on scroll.
- **Mocks:** agent swimlanes for the AI tools (a logic-analyzer view: 8 lanes, mono ids, state segments, the time axis is just the visitor's own time on the page, labelled so), watch list, live call card, trader room.
- **Treatment:** warmer and more editorial; quote cards become pull quotes between sections.

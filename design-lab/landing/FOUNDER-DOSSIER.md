- **Personal outcomes are his story, never a promise.** No personal money figure is ever a headline, a callout, a highlighted number or a "result". Under any founder story that mentions an outcome, use the footnote: *My story, not a typical result and not a promise of yours.*
- **Never merge two tellings into a new number.** See section 5.

How it works

  1. Search for a coin, or browse a category and tap the coins you want. They wait in Unranked until you rank them.
  2. Drag a coin onto a tier, or tap it and pick a tier. On a phone, press and hold a coin to drag it.
  3. Export an image or copy a link. Your list saves in this browser.

EXTENDED BIO

Jesus Martinez is a Miami-based creator and the voice behind JM Crypto. A first-generation Cuban American, he entered crypto in 2021 after his brother lost his life savings through high-leverage trading. He put $700 into Axie Infinity and, through the game's breeding market, grew that into mid-five figures, helping his brother recover and setting himself on a new path.

Full time in crypto since 2021, Jesus now shares research, project deep dives, and interviews through JM Crypto. His interests span the wider crypto market, Bittensor, AI, and the relationship between attention and capital. His focus is growing JM Crypto and making what he learns useful to the people watching.

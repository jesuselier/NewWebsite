Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

This page explains what information Swarm collects, why we need it, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version

  • We collect as little as we can.
  • Our website counts visits without cookies.
  • The intro video loads from YouTube only after you press play.
  • Whop handles payments. We never see your full card or bank number.
  • Discord hosts the community, so Discord's privacy policy covers what you do there.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • Email us anytime to see, fix or delete what we hold about you.

01Who we are

Swarm is a paid AI crypto trading community run by Jesus Martinez in Miami, Florida. "We" and "us" mean the operator of Swarm.

This policy covers the website at www.joinswarm.ai, the Swarm Discord server, the weekly live calls and the AI tools we give members.

02When you visit the website

Hosting

The website is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

Visit counts

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies. It records things like the page you viewed, the site that sent you, your country, and your browser, operating system and device type. We see totals, not a profile of you.

The intro video

The intro video comes from youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing loads from YouTube until you press play. Once you press play, YouTube (owned by Google) receives information such as your IP address and may store data in your browser, under Google's privacy policy.

Fonts and code libraries

The site serves its fonts and its 3D graphics library from its own host, so no font or code service learns about your visit.

Links to other sites

Links on the site to YouTube, X or Discord take you to those services, and their own privacy policies apply there.

The website does not use advertising cookies or ad tracking pixels.

03When you join and pay

Checkout runs through Whop. Whop collects your payment details (card or bank account for ACH) and processes the payment. We never see your full card or bank account number.

Whop shares with us what we need to run your membership, such as your name, email address, Whop username, plan, payment status and renewal dates. If you connect your Discord account through Whop to get member access, Whop also shares your Discord username with us.

Whop's own privacy policy covers what Whop does with your information.

04Inside Discord and on live calls

Swarm lives on Discord. Discord collects and stores your account details, messages and activity under its own privacy policy.

Inside the Swarm server, Jesus and anyone who helps him run it can see what you post, the same way other members can. We may also see basic server activity, such as when you joined.

The weekly live calls run in the Swarm Discord. Everyone on the call can see and hear what you say or share, including your portfolio if you show it during a live review. Calls may be recorded and shared with members. Only share what you are comfortable showing.

05The AI tools and email

When you use the AI tools, we process what you enter so the tool can work. Please don't enter passwords, private keys, seed phrases or other secrets into any tool.

If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write, and we use it to answer you.

06How we use information

  • To give you access to Swarm and manage your membership.
  • To run the calls, the tools and member support.
  • To see how many people visit the website and which pages help.
  • To tell you about your membership, price changes and updates to our policies.
  • To keep the community safe and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.

We never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or exchange passwords.

07Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run Swarm, and only what they need:

  • Whop for checkout, payments and membership.
  • Discord for the community.
  • Vercel for website hosting and visit counts.
  • Google for YouTube, which plays the intro video after you press play.
  • jsDelivr for a code library the website loads.
  • AI providers that power the tools, as described in section 05.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, when we need their help.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect members or the community, or as part of a sale or transfer of Swarm. If Swarm changes hands, we will tell members.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

08How long we keep it

  • Membership and payment records: while you are a member, and after that for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Website visit counts: kept only as totals.
  • Discord messages: until you or a moderator delete them, or Discord removes them.
  • Emails: as long as we need them to help you.

09Your choices and rights

  • Email us to see what we hold about you, fix it, or ask us to delete it. Write from the email address on your membership so we can confirm it's you. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • You can delete your own Discord messages, leave the server, or cancel your plan in Whop at any time.
  • Whop and Discord keep their own copies of your account data. To delete those, use their account settings or contact them directly.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the European Union or the United Kingdom), you may have more rights, such as the right to object or to get a copy of your data. We will honor them as your law requires.

We may need to keep some records even after you ask us to delete them, for example payment records that tax law requires us to keep.

We are based in the United States. The services we use may store and process information in the United States and other countries.

10Security

We use well known providers and limit who on our side can see member information. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your Whop and Discord accounts with a strong password and two-factor sign-in.

11Children

Swarm is only for people 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. For big changes, we will also post a notice in the Swarm Discord.
